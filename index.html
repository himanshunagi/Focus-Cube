First time ever picked up
No memory yet — shows 00:00. LED ring glows softly to signal "ready". Dial on left to set your time. Nothing else to do.
Sensor: IR proximity detects hand within ~3cm. Display wakes in under 0.2 seconds. Battery-efficient — sensor uses under 0.5mA standby.

Dial rotating clockwise
Dial to select time
Each click of the dial = 1 minute. Clockwise adds time, anticlockwise removes it. Ring fills proportionally to show the selected duration. Display updates live.
Feel: Tactile notch per minute (like a watch crown). After 2 seconds of no movement, selection is confirmed silently — no button press needed to confirm time.
Session started!
Timer hidden · hourglass visible
Button pressed
Timer vanishes → Hourglass appears
The moment Start is pressed, the digital clock disappears completely. The hourglass takes over. No numbers. No countdown stress. Just sand flowing down at the pace of your chosen time.
Why this matters: Watching a countdown (14:32… 14:31…) triggers anxiety. The hourglass shows progress without pressure — you see roughly how much is left, not exactly. This is intentional.

Sand flows, ring fills
Top half empties, bottom half fills. LED ring arc grows clockwise around the front face. No numbers anywhere on screen. The hourglass IS the timer.
XP reminder: Complete without pressing Break = 200 XP · Complete with Break = 100 XP · End early = 20 XP
SESSION COMPLETE
+200 XP · Sleeping in 2min
Sand empty from top
Top empty = session done
All sand in the bottom half. LED ring completes its full arc and pulses green. Haptic vibrates once. XP awarded. After exactly 2 minutes, the cube goes to sleep automatically.
Sleep behaviour: Display dims slowly over 10 seconds, then off. Sensor remains active at ultra-low power to detect next pickup.
Remembered from last time
Dial to change · Press to keep
Memory active
Picks up where you left off
Cube wakes up and immediately shows your last used time — no dialling needed if you want the same session. Just press Start. If you want a different time, dial to change it first.
Memory logic: Stores last confirmed start time in flash memory. Persists through sleep, battery swap, and power cycles. If you used 20 min last time, it shows 20:00 on every subsequent pickup until you change it.

Left Side — Dial Only
One job: set the time
Removed the sound toggle from here — that's now on the right side. The dial gets the whole panel. Easier to grip, better leverage.

Removed preset time buttons
5/10/15/25min presets are gone — the dial handles all time setting now. Right side gets: Vol+, Vol−, and a sound mode toggle button that cycles through vibrate → silent → sound.
